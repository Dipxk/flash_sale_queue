The problem this solves

When everyone buys at once, shops break.

Flash sales create two failures: overselling (two people get the last item) and meltdown (checkout collapses under the spike). THRESHOLD puts shoppers in a fair waiting room, admits them in controlled batches, and locks inventory so stock never goes negative.
